Support & FAQ

Requirements

  • Your own self-hosted Frigate NVR server. Elsinore does not include, host, or sell an NVR — you need one running already.
  • Frigate 0.17.x or later is the actively tested version. Older versions may work for basic viewing but are not verified.
  • A way to reach your Frigate server's address from your iPhone, iPad, or Mac: the same local network, or a remote path such as a Tailscale/VPN overlay network (recommended) or your own reverse proxy.

Setting up the app

  1. Open Elsinore and choose “Add Server.”
  2. Enter your Frigate server's base URL (for example, http://192.168.1.50:8971 on a local network, or a Tailscale MagicDNS name such as https://nvr.your-tailnet.ts.net for remote access).
  3. Enter your Frigate username and password (native Frigate auth). Your credential is stored in the system Keychain (iOS, iPadOS, or macOS) and never sent anywhere except to the server you entered.
  4. Once connected, the live grid, review feed, and clip playback should populate directly from your server.

Remote access

The recommended way to use Elsinore away from home is an overlay network such as Tailscale or another WireGuard-based VPN. Install the same overlay network's app on both your Frigate server's host and your iPhone, iPad, or Mac, then use the tailnet address (a 100.64.0.0/10 address or a .ts.net MagicDNS name) as your server URL in Elsinore. This keeps your camera traffic off the public internet and typically allows low-latency live video (WebRTC) to work remotely, which a plain reverse-proxy setup often cannot guarantee.

A public reverse proxy with your own TLS certificate is also supported, but live video may fall back to higher-latency HLS instead of WebRTC depending on what your proxy allows through.

Investigate

Investigate follows one object across every camera at once. Open an event's details from the dossier card's i tile, then tap Investigate this moment — the timeline opens at the moment the scrubber is sitting on, not at the event's start.

  • Drag anywhere over the lanes to move the cursor. One cursor runs through every camera, so all the lanes move together.
  • Tap a bar to follow that sighting: the camera becomes the focused one, the cursor jumps to where the bar begins, and the preview above shows that camera's frame at that instant. Tap a camera's name to switch to it without moving the cursor.
  • A bar drawn faintly is something that has been sitting still for more than half an hour — a parked car, not a passer-by. A small chevron on a bar's left edge means the sighting began before the visible window.
  • Where next lists the cameras the object is likely to appear on and roughly when. Tap a row to jump there; swipe it for Same / Not same to teach the grouping.
  • Follow at the bottom left plays the whole visit back for you: each sighting in order, switching cameras by itself, so you watch someone move through the property without picking cameras. The controls under the picture step between cameras, pause, and toggle 2× speed; if a camera has no recording for its stretch, Follow says so and moves on to the next one.
  • Previous / Next sighting at the bottom walk the same visit from camera to camera, and Open camera hands the moment back to the ordinary scrub reel.
  • The capsule on the left of the time axis (10 min) cycles how much time the window shows.

Troubleshooting

  • Can't connect / login fails. Double-check the server URL, including the port, and confirm you can reach that same address from a browser on the same device.
  • Live view works but feels delayed. This usually means the app has fallen back to HLS because low-latency WebRTC media couldn't reach your device (common on restrictive networks or plain reverse proxies without an overlay network). This is expected graceful-degradation behavior, not a bug.
  • Notifications aren't arriving. Push notifications require the optional companion “sidecar” component to be running alongside your Frigate server, and notification permission to be granted to the app in iOS Settings. See the Privacy Policy for how notifications work.
  • App can't reach my server after a network change. Confirm your device's own network path (Wi-Fi, cellular, VPN/tailnet connection) can currently reach the server address you configured.
