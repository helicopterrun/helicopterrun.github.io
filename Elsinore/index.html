03 / Private by design

Your home isn't a feed.
It stays yours.

[ 01 ]

No account. No cloud.

Elsinore talks straight to your Frigate server, at home or from anywhere. There is no Elsinore cloud in the middle and nothing to sign up for.

[ 02 ]

No tracking.

No analytics, no telemetry, no advertising or tracking SDKs. Your password stays in the device's secure storage and only ever goes to your server.

[ 03 ]

Your video stays home.

Footage and notification images come from your own server. Optional notifications carry one short line through the system push service. Privacy policy

04 / What you need

Before you
download.

[ 01 ]

Your own Frigate server

Elsinore is a viewer and reviewer for an existing Frigate setup. Frigate 0.17 or later is the version we test against. If you don't run Frigate, Elsinore won't be useful to you.

[ 02 ]

A way to reach it

Your home network, or a remote path of your own: Tailscale, another WireGuard VPN, or a reverse proxy.

[ 03 ]

Optional: Marcellus

A free, open-source companion service that runs next to Frigate. It adds alerts, live lock-screen alerts, encounters, Explore and scrub previews. Without it you still get live view, recordings and the review feed. About Marcellus

05 / Good to know

A few things,
before you look.

What is Elsinore?

A fast, native app for Frigate, the open-source NVR you run on your own hardware. It brings your live cameras, recordings, encounters and alerts to iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Is there a subscription?

No subscription and no account. One App Store purchase covers iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Does my video leave my network?

Video, recordings and snapshots go straight from your server to your device. The one exception is optional notifications: the short line on your lock screen, such as “Person at Front Door”, travels through the system push service and a minimal relay with no database. The privacy policy has the details.

Can I use it away from home?

Yes, over any route you already have to your server: Tailscale, a WireGuard VPN, or your own reverse proxy. If it can't connect, the Connection Doctor tells you why.

Does it replace Frigate's own interface?

No. Elsinore doesn't configure cameras or replace Frigate's admin pages. It's for watching, scrubbing and reviewing.

Where do I get help?

Start with Getting started and the documentation. The support page covers quick answers and how to report a bug.
