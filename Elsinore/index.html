Elsinore

Native client for Frigate NVR

Elsinore connects to a Frigate server you run and control — on your own network, or reachable remotely over an overlay network such as Tailscale. The app talks to your server and nowhere else.

What it does

Live grid

Every camera at once, with low-latency WebRTC when you open one up.

Scrub reel

A film-strip timeline of the day — drag through hours of footage frame by frame.

Review feed

Alerts and detections with filters, object tracks, and clip playback.

Smart push

Optional notifications that escalate, resolve, and never leak your footage.

Clip export

Pull a clip straight out of your recordings and share it.

Instrument, not document

A dark, monospaced interface built for watching — five palettes, one discipline.

The deal

  • No Elsinore account.
  • No cloud backend.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking SDKs.
  • Your video never leaves your own infrastructure.
