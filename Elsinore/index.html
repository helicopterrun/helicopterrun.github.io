Elsinore

A native iOS client for your own self-hosted Frigate NVR.

Elsinore connects to a Frigate server you run and control — on your own network, or reachable remotely over an overlay network such as Tailscale. There is no Elsinore account, no cloud backend, and no bundled or third-party analytics. The app talks to your server and nowhere else.

It gives you a live camera grid, a scrollable review feed of alerts and detections, and clip playback — all read directly from your own Frigate instance.
