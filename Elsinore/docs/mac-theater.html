Mac Theater

On this page
  • The layout
  • Hover thumbnails
  • Right-click the video
  • Pop-out windows
  • Keyboard shortcuts
  • Shortcuts and Siri

In a wide Mac window, the Scrub page switches to Theater: the video as large as the window allows, with a strip of object lanes underneath instead of the phone's drum. Windows narrower than about 900 points, or taller than they are wide, keep the iPhone-style stacked layout. This page covers what Theater adds. The reel itself works as described in Scrub.

The layout

  • The video fills the window above the strip, with the transport bar (play, speed, mute, export) under it.
  • The strip shows one row per object type (such as person or vehicle), with event bars drawn on each row. Click a row's label to turn that object lane on or off; right-click a label for Show Only or Show All. Click a bar to jump to that event's start.
  • Previous, Next and LIVE sit at the ends of the strip.
  • A floating event card appears bottom-left on the video while the cursor is on an event, and is hidden otherwise. Click it for the event detail.
  • In Explore, the same strip also carries one row per camera that saw the subject.

Hover thumbnails

Hold the pointer over the strip and a small preview of that moment appears above it. It uses preview frames the app has already downloaded, so it shows nothing for a moment it has no frame for, and it hides while you are scrubbing.

Right-click the video

Right-click (or Control-click) the picture for:

  • Copy Still and Save Still..., which take the exact frame on screen when the recorded player has one.
  • Share Still..., available when there is a frame to share.
  • Export 30 s Around Here: a clip from 15 seconds before to 15 seconds after the moment, never reaching past now.
  • Explore This Moment.
  • Open in New Window, described below.
  • Jump to Live.

You can also drag the picture out of the window onto the Desktop, a Finder folder, Messages or Mail. It arrives as a JPEG. Dragging works only during recorded playback; at live there is no recorded frame to take.

Pop-out windows

Reel > Open Scrub in New Window (or the right-click item) opens that camera's Scrub page in its own window, with its own reel and player. There is one pop-out per camera, and at most four open at once; when the limit is reached the menu item reads "Open in New Window (4 open)" and is disabled. A pop-out has no further pop-outs of its own.

Separately, a camera can open as a live window. Both kinds of window have a Float on Top toggle (the pin in the toolbar; Command-Shift-P in the live window) that keeps the window above other apps.

Keyboard shortcuts

Press ? on the Scrub page for a shortcut sheet, listing every command in groups: Moving, Events, Zoom & view, Playback, Stills & export and Cameras. Escape or a click outside closes it. ? is ignored while you are typing in a field. The same sheet opens from Help > Scrub Keyboard Shortcuts (Command-/). The full command list is on Elsinore on Mac.

Shortcuts and Siri

Elsinore provides an Open Camera action to the Shortcuts app, Spotlight and Siri, on iPhone, iPad and Mac. It opens a camera live, or at a moment if you give it a time. The spoken phrases are:

  • "Show camera in Elsinore"
  • "Open camera in Elsinore"
  • "Scrub camera in Elsinore"
