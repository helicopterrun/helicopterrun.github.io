A note on the sidecar

Elsinore works against a bare Frigate server: live, scrub, and the search feed all come from Frigate itself. Push notifications, Live Activities, encounters, and Investigate come from the optional Marcellus sidecar. Pages that describe sidecar-only behaviour say so at the top.
