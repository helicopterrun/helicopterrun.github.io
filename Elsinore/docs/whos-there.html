People (Who's There)

On this page
  • IDs and names
  • Finding People
  • A person's page
  • Merge suggestions
  • Review tab filter
  • Privacy

People is the app's view of the faces your Marcellus sidecar has recognized. It is not part of plain Frigate, and it appears only when the server says it supports it: the sidecar must have face recognition enabled. If the server does not offer it, the app shows nothing for it, with no empty page.

IDs and names

Every face the sidecar groups together gets a permanent number, shown as #11. A name is an optional label on that number. You never need a name to see, follow, search or correct a face; an unnamed face is simply #11.

IDs are never reused. When two IDs turn out to be the same person and you merge them, the old number keeps working and points at the survivor.

Finding People

  • iPhone and iPad: a People strip of faces sits at the top of the Search tab; tap one to open it, or open the full list from the strip.
  • Mac: People is in the sidebar.

The list groups faces as Today, Following, Who is this?, Named, and Seen once.

A person's page

The page shows when the face was Last recognized, a Rhythm summary of when they tend to appear, and their visits as strips of face crops. Tap a visit to open that footage, and Previous and Next then move between that person's visits. The wording is deliberate: "Last recognized", never "last here". Most visits show no usable face, so the app only knows when the face was recognized, not when someone was actually present.

The Actions menu on the page offers:

  • Name (or Rename).
  • Follow or Unfollow. Unnamed, unfollowed IDs are forgotten after the server's expiry period; following keeps one.
  • Add note (or Edit note).
  • Merge.
  • Delete, which erases their face crops and history from your server.

Within a visit you can also mark a sighting as not this person, which removes it from them; the page offers an undo.

Merge suggestions

When two IDs look like one person, a Might be the same person row appears at the top of the list. Each suggestion shows the faces side by side. Merge combines them (and fails if both already have different names); you can also say they are not the same, and that pair is not suggested again. Splitting one person into several IDs is the more common fault, so this is how existing IDs get tidied up.

Review tab filter

On the Review tab, the Person button in the toolbar narrows the encounters to one person. It clears when you relaunch the app. A person's page also has a Recent encounters row that opens the same feed for them. Review needs the sidecar's encounters; see Search and Review.

Privacy

Face crops, IDs, names and recognition data stay on your server. Names, handles and face IDs are not included in push notifications and never reach the push relay or Apple's push service: a recognized person's alert reads the same as an unrecognized one's. Recognition can still change how an alert is routed, but not its words. See the privacy policy.
