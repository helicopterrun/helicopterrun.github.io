The Mac app is the same app, built from the same code, with the chrome a Mac expects.

Elsinore ships as a Universal Purchase: the Mac app shares its bundle identifier with the iPhone and iPad app, so one purchase covers both.

What is shared

Live, Scrub, Search and Review, Investigate and Follow, and the whole alert routing model are the same views and the same logic on both platforms. There is no Mac-only subset of features; the differences are in input and in chrome.

What differs

No Live Activities. Live Activities and the Dynamic Island are an iPhone and iPad feature. On Mac, Elsinore delivers ordinary notifications plus a menu bar companion. The Alerts settings say so on Mac rather than showing a switch that would do nothing. Notifications, quiet hours, sounds, and the attention ladder all work.

Separate camera windows. A camera can be opened in its own window, which is the thing a Mac can do that a phone cannot.

A menu bar companion, for status at a glance without bringing the app forward.

A transport bar under the reel, instead of the controls shade the phone uses. Trackpad and mouse drive the reel alongside the keyboard.

Server › Guide. The Mac app embeds the sidecar's own /guide documentation directly, with your session already attached so it does not land you on a login page. See the operator manual.

Settings tabs rather than a list: General, Connection, Appearance, Alerts, Storage, Scrub Feel, and About. General carries Launch at login; Scrub Feel is the reel tuning that lives in the controls shade on iOS. See the settings reference.

Handoff

Viewing a camera publishes a Handoff activity carrying the camera name and the playback time, so the camera and moment you are looking at on one Apple device can be picked up on another.
